# rule: research integrity (always on)

> **What this file is:** the anti-hallucination rule for product/market
> work. The most dangerous discovery failure is confident invented
> analytics — a fake market size reads exactly like a real one.

- Every market, competitor, pricing or user-behavior claim carries a source
  URL and an as-of date, or the explicit mark **(assumption, unverified)**.
  No third option.
- Numbers are never invented, averaged from memory, or "estimated" without
  showing the basis. An honest "no data found" beats a plausible figure.
- Sources get spot-checked: a claim whose URL doesn't actually support it
  is removed, not softened.
- Model knowledge counts as memory, not as a source — it can suggest where
  to look, it cannot be cited.
- Unverified claims that decisions depend on go to
  `framework/docs/product/assumptions.md` with a validation plan — they don't get
  promoted to facts by repetition.
